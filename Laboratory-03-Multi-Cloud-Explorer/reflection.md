# Mission 3 Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** impressed me the most because of its strong focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and Kubernetes. I also found it interesting how GCP provides services that can support both simple applications and large-scale systems. However, I learned that there is no single cloud provider that is always the best for every situation.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide important cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, and identity management. They also allow organizations to scale their resources depending on their needs. Even though the services have different names, many of them perform similar functions, which made it easier for me to compare the three platforms.

Business requirements have a major effect on cloud platform selection. A company needs to consider its budget, existing technologies, performance requirements, security, scalability, and the type of applications it plans to run. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an AI research company may prefer GCP because of its AI and machine learning capabilities.

By acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about choosing the most popular platform. I need to understand the client's actual problem first and then match their requirements with the services offered by each provider. The Linux investigation also helped me understand how a server running on a local or virtual environment could be moved to cloud virtual machine services such as Amazon EC2, Azure Virtual Machines, and Google Compute Engine.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved during this mission. I added organized Markdown documentation, comparison tables, recommendations, research about different cloud providers, and screenshots as evidence of my work. Overall, this mission helped me better understand how cloud technologies are applied to real business situations.
